One Body in Christ

A Spiritual Family to Walk Beside You.

Membership in our fellowship is not an administrative label, but a shared commitment of mutual prayer, discipleship, and active service in sharing the Gospel.

What Membership Means

The Four Privileges & Commitments of Membership

Whether you attend a local home church or join our online global community, you belong here.

1

Covenant of Daily Prayer

Every member is covered by name in our pastoral intercessory prayers. In turn, members commit to praying for salvation decisions and community prayer requests weekly.

2

Monthly Discipleship Circles

Exclusive online monthly gatherings featuring live teaching from Pastor Jonathan, guest theologians, Q&A on Christian living, and small group breakout fellowship.

3

Personal Mentor Connection

Get matched with a seasoned, compassionate mentor who can check in on your spiritual growth, pray through family challenges, and encourage your walk in grace.

4

Frontline Mission Access

Receive confidential prayer bulletins from remote mission fields, early volunteer registration for citywide crusades, and free digital study materials.
