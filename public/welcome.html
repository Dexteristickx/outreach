Heaven is Rejoicing

Welcome home, brother or sister!

You made the most profound choice in human existence today. The Bible declares that all of heaven rejoices right now over your decision.

What Just Happened in the Spiritual Realm?

  • ✓ Total Forgiveness: Every single sin, past, present, and future, has been wiped from God's ledger through the blood of Jesus (Colossians 2:14).
  • ✓ Divine Adoption: You are not merely a servant; you are legally and spiritually adopted as God's beloved son or daughter (Romans 8:15).
  • ✓ The Holy Spirit's Indwelling: God’s own Spirit now lives inside you to comfort, guide, empower, and teach you truth (Ephesians 1:13).
  • ✓ Eternal Security: Your future is irrevocably held in Christ's triumphant hand (John 10:28-29).
First 24 Hours

Your Immediate Next Steps

Don't worry about trying to master theology today. Take these four gentle steps:

1

Whisper a Simple Thank You

Before you go to sleep tonight, take 30 seconds to say: "Jesus, thank You for loving me and making me new." Prayer is simply an honest conversation with your loving Father.

2

Check Your Email Inbox

We have prepared our warm 7-Day Welcome Nurture series for you. Day 1 is on its way with gentle, practical guidance for your first week with Jesus.

3

Start the Gospel of John

Read just one chapter of John each day. It was written by one of Jesus’s closest earthly companions to show you who Jesus is up close.

4

Explore the New Believers Hub

We created interactive tools including an interactive 30-Day Bible plan, an ACTS prayer model guide, and advice on finding a loving local church.
