Weekly Rhythms

Daily Intercessory Points

Join our church family in praying together across the days of the week.

Monday Focus

Eyes for God’s Faithfulness

"Lord Jesus, open our eyes to notice Your goodness in the small details. Where anxiety threatens to rush in, anchor our thoughts in Your peace."

Wednesday Focus

Healing & Provision

"Father of mercies, supply every need according to Your riches in glory. Comfort those battling bodily pain and financial stress."

Friday Focus

Courage to Share the Gospel

"Holy Spirit, grant us tender hearts for our neighbors who do not yet know Christ. Let our words be seasoned with grace and unconditional love."
