I Need Prayer
A Sacred Moment

The most important decision you will ever make.

You do not need to clean up your life before you come to Jesus. You come broken, weary, or hesitant, and He makes all things new.

Salvation is not about joining a religious club, memorizing ritual chants, or pretending you have everything figured out. It is an intentional, heartfelt turning to God, recognizing our need for His forgiveness, and trusting Jesus Christ as Savior and Lord.

Jesus paid the full debt of our past, present, and future failures at the Cross. The moment you invite Him into your heart, you are immediately forgiven, welcomed into God's eternal family, and sealed with the Holy Spirit.

There is no magical formula in the exact words. What God looks at is the sincerity of your heart. If you are ready, read this prayer slowly and make it the declaration of your soul:

A Prayer of Surrender
"Lord Jesus, I need You. I admit that I am a sinner and have lived life on my own terms. Thank You for loving me enough to die on the cross for my sins and rising from the dead to give me life.

Right now, I open the door of my heart. I receive You as my Savior, my Lord, and my Friend. Wash away my guilt, take my anxiety and shame, and lead my steps from this day forward.

Thank You for forgiving me and making me a child of God. In Jesus’ name, Amen."
