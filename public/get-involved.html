Serve With Us

Join the Outreach Care Team

We are looking for humble, compassionate disciples to serve in three key areas:

🙏

1. Intercessory Prayer Team

Receive confidential daily prayer requests submitted by seekers in crisis, family struggles, or addiction, and lift them by name in intercession.

💌

2. New Believer Follow-Up Responders

Trained believers who reply with personal encouragement to individuals who submitted the salvation decision form, answering early questions and recommending local churches.

🌍

3. Local Church Connectors

Help us vet and curate healthy, loving congregations in various cities and countries so new believers have a safe spiritual family to attend.
