Core Curriculum

4 Foundational Training Modules

Practical, theological, and field-tested curriculum for every believer called to reach the lost.

1

The Gospel Foundation

Deep study of Christology, the finished work of the Cross, and salvation by grace through faith. Learn to articulate the Good News with theological accuracy and clarity, free of moralism or legalistic confusion.

  • The Biblical Storyline (Creation, Fall, Redemption, Restoration)
  • Justification, Sanctification & Eternal Security
  • Christ-Centered Scripture Exegesis
2

Compassionate Personal Evangelism

Evangelism is not an argument to win; it is a person to love. Learn the art of deep listening, asking gentle diagnostic questions, and overcoming secular skepticism with warmth and intellectual rigor.

  • Starting Spiritual Conversations Naturally
  • Answering Doubts (Suffering, Science, Pluralism)
  • Leading a Seeker Through the Prayer of Salvation
3

Discipleship & Follow-Up Care

Salvation is the starting line. Master the crucial art of post-decision follow-up: walking with new believers through their first 30 days, guarding them against early disillusionment, and integrating them into healthy local churches.

  • The First 24-Hour Follow-Up Protocol
  • Leading 1-on-1 Discipleship & Discovery Bible Studies
  • Recognizing & Navigating Spiritual Warfare
4

Cross-Cultural & Field Missions

Preparing for short-term and long-term missionary service. Learn cultural humility, urban street evangelism logistics, campus revival planning, and rural unreached community engagement.

  • Cross-Cultural Adaptation & Contextualization
  • Open-Air Proclamation & Sound Logistics
  • Compassion Care, Feeding & Medical Outreach Integration
