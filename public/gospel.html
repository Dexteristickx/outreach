Accept Jesus
The Good News

Christianity is not merely a moral code; it is a living relationship with Jesus Christ.

Religion says: "Work, strive, and perhaps God will accept you." Jesus says: "Come to me all who are weary, and I will give you rest. It is finished."

Historical & Living Reality

Who Is Jesus?

He was neither a folklore myth nor an ordinary moral rabbi. Discover the singular figure who divided human history into BC and AD.

1

A Real Person in History

Jesus was an eyewitness reality in Roman-occupied Judea. Non-Christian ancient historians, including Tacitus, Suetonius, and Josephus, independently corroborate His life, impact, and execution under Pontius Pilate outside Jerusalem.

2

Unrivaled Compassion

In a world of harsh divisions, Jesus touched untouchable lepers, defended widows, forgave the condemned, and restored human dignity to foreigners and outcasts without demanding a prior moral pedigree.

3

God in Human Flesh

Jesus claimed authority that belongs to God alone: to forgive human sins, command the stormy seas, and raise the dead. As C.S. Lewis famously said, He cannot be considered merely a good moral teacher: He is either deceitful, delusional, or truly the Lord.

4

The Empty Tomb

Crucified in public, His tomb was found empty on Sunday morning. Over 500 eyewitnesses interacted with Him physically alive, sparking a fearless revolution of grace that transformed the Roman Empire.

Deep Human Questions

Why Does This Matter to You Today?

Every human heart quietly asks three fundamental questions in the stillness of midnight: "Am I truly loved?", "Can my regrets be undone?", and "What happens when I breathe my last?"

1. The Exhaustion of Proving Your Worth

Modern life whispers that your worth equals your performance, your income, your followers, or your morality. But the finish line always shifts. Jesus offers unconditional acceptance before you do a single good deed. In Him, you rest in an identity that cannot be revoked.

2. Complete Freedom from Guilt & Shame

We all carry memories we wish we could erase and secret guilt that makes us feel unlovable. Christianity is the only message in the world where God does not ask you to balance your bad deeds with good ones on a scale. Jesus took your full record of failures upon Himself at the Cross and paid it in full.

3. An Anchor Beyond the Grave

Because Jesus conquered death and rose bodily, death has lost its sting. For those who belong to Christ, our ultimate future is not annihilation or darkness, but an eternal reunion in a renewed creation with no more tears, cancer, or separation.

Clear & Step-by-Step

The Plan of Salvation

God’s rescue story is neither complicated nor cryptic. Follow the five foundational steps of the Gospel.

1

God’s Boundless Love

God created you with purpose and care to know His love, experience His peace, and walk in relationship with Him.

"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life." John 3:16
2

The Broken Connection (Sin)

Every one of us has rebelled against God’s design, choosing selfishness and independence. This is what the Bible calls sin. Just as a lamp unplugged from its socket goes dark, unplugging ourselves from God brought spiritual death.

"For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God. The wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord." Romans 3:23 & 6:23
3

The Unbreakable Bridge (The Cross)

No amount of human charity, philosophy, or religious ritual could bridge the canyon between our imperfection and God's absolute holiness. So God bridged it Himself. Jesus took our penalty upon Himself and died in our place.

"God shows his love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us." Romans 5:8
4

Grace as a Free Gift

Salvation is not an achievement; it is a gift to be received with open hands. You don't get clean before you take a shower; you step into the shower to be cleansed. Come as you are.

"For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast." Ephesians 2:8-9
5

Your Personal Decision

Jesus does not force open the door of your heart. He stands at the threshold and knocks. Salvation becomes yours the moment you consciously trust Him as your Lord and Savior.

"If you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved." Romans 10:9

Faith is not shutting off your mind. We welcome your hardest intellectual and emotional questions.

Yet uniquely in Christianity, God did not observe human anguish from a detached ivory throne; in Jesus, God plunged into human pain, was betrayed, tortured, and killed unjustly. He knows the agony of loss firsthand, and He promises to one day eradicate evil and wipe every tear from our eyes.

While religions often share commendable moral guidelines (be kind, don't steal), their core answers to who God is and how humans are reconciled to Him are contradictory. Eastern religions envision an impersonal cosmos with cyclical rebirth; Islam teaches salvation based on an individual's deeds outweighing misdeeds. Jesus alone taught that humans are spiritually dead and cannot save themselves. Salvation is not a ladder we climb by human effort; it is God reaching down in grace to rescue us.

The scientific revolution was founded largely by devout Christian thinkers, including Sir Isaac Newton, Johannes Kepler, Blaise Pascal, Robert Boyle, and Francis Bacon, who believed that an orderly, rational God created an intelligible universe governed by discernible natural laws. Science explores physical mechanisms (how the clock ticks); theology reveals the Clockmaker and the purpose of life.
Doubt is not the opposite of faith; stubborn pride is. In Mark 9:24, a father cried out to Jesus with raw tears: 'Lord, I believe; help my unbelief!'
